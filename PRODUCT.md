# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ECHO PROTOCOL players and administrators using the web portal to manage accounts, payments, gameplay configuration, and operational data.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy companion and administration surface for the cooperative horror game, with fast access to operational workflows and clear system state.

## Brand Personality

Dark, tense, authoritative. The horror identity should frame the product without obstructing tasks or reducing readability.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative gore, distracting ambient animation, novelty controls, and effects that cover navigation or data. Do not turn the admin portal into a cinematic game screen.

## Design Principles

- Keep operational content and controls visually dominant.
- Preserve the dark horror identity through restrained color and typography.
- Use motion only to communicate state or feedback.
- Prefer familiar, consistent product controls over decorative novelty.
- Make system status and destructive actions unambiguous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, and reduced-motion support.
